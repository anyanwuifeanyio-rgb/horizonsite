## 1. INTRODUCTION & PLATFORM OVERVIEW

The **Horizon Move Limited** web platform is an enterprise-grade travel, education, and immigration management system. Designed for Nigerian professionals, students, business travelers, and families, the platform combines live airline Global Distribution Systems (GDS) with structured academic admissions and European employment permit consulting.

### Core System Architecture
- **Framework:** React 18+ with TypeScript & Vite
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS with responsive desktop and mobile design
- **Flight Engine:** Integrated SkyLink GDS engine supporting real-time seat queries, fare calculation, multi-currency switching, and automated PNR reservation handling.
- **Client Storage:** Secure local and session caching for pending searches, consultation leads, and active ticket records.

## 3. HOW TO USE THE FLIGHT ENGINE (PRIMARY SERVICE)

Because international air ticketing is the most widely utilized service, the flight engine is positioned prominently on both the homepage and the dedicated Travel Bookings page.

### 3.1 Searching for Flights
1. **Locate the Flight Engine:** On the homepage, scroll just beneath the top Trust Bar, or click the **"Book Flights (Live GDS)"** button in the hero header.
2. **Select Trip Type:** Choose between **Round Trip**, **One Way**, or **Multi-City**.
3. **Choose Departure & Destination:**
   - Default departure is set to **Lagos (LOS) - Murtala Muhammed Int'l**.
   - Select your destination from the quick-pick badges (London LHR, Manchester MAN, Toronto YYZ, Dubai DXB, New York JFK, Doha DOH, Istanbul IST, Nairobi NBO) or type any global 3-letter IATA airport code.
4. **Select Travel Dates:** Enter departure and return dates. The engine automatically flags peak and off-peak travel seasons.
5. **Specify Passengers & Luggage:**
   - Select number of Adults, Children, and Infants.
   - **Student Baggage Option:** Toggle the *Student 46kg Luggage Allowance* to filter for airlines offering 2x 23kg check-in bags free of charge (Qatar Airways, Virgin Atlantic, British Airways, Ethiopian Airlines).
6. **Execute Search:** Click **"Search Live GDS Flights"**. Results load immediately with verifiable flight numbers, transit times, baggage allocations, and all-inclusive taxes.

### 3.2 Booking & Reserving a Flight
1. Review flight offers from Qatar Airways, Emirates, British Airways, Air Peace, and Virgin Atlantic.
2. Click **"Select & Book"** or **"Reserve Seat"**.
3. Enter passenger passport details:
   - Full Legal Name (matching International Passport)
   - Date of Birth & Gender
   - Passport Number & Expiry Date
   - Contact Phone & Email
4. Choose Payment Method:
   - **Direct Bank Transfer (Zenith Bank / Stanbic IBTC Escrow)**
   - **Nigerian Debit/Credit Card (Mastercard, Visa, Verve)**
   - **Embassy Visa Reservation Only (PNR Itinerary):** Generates an official verifiable reservation document for visa submission without buying the ticket outright.
5. Receive your instant confirmed booking reference with six-character PNR code.

## 4. HOW TO USE THE STUDY ABROAD MODULE

1. Navigate to **Study Abroad** from the top menu.
2. Use the **Country Filter** to compare universities in the United Kingdom, Canada, United States, and European destinations (Germany, France, Ireland, Cyprus).
3. Check the **English Proficiency Requirement**: Look for the *WAEC/NECO C6+ Accepted* badge to avoid taking IELTS/TOEFL examinations.
4. Review Tuition Ranges: Use the currency selector in the top bar to display tuition in NGN (₦), GBP (£), USD ($), or EUR (€).
5. Click **"Apply to Study Abroad (2026/27 Intake)"** to launch the digital application intake form, or click **"Book Academic Consultation"** to speak with an educational counselor.

## 5. HOW TO APPLY FOR WORK PERMITS

1. Navigate to **Work Permits** from the navigation menu.
2. Browse active government-sanctioned employment quotas by country:
   - **Poland:** Manufacturing, Logistics, Food Processing (1-3 year TRC)
   - **Czech Republic:** Automotive Assembly, Warehousing, Hospitality
   - **Serbia & Albania:** Construction, Heavy Machinery, Agriculture
   - **Lithuania:** Transport Logistics, Long-haul Driving
3. Click on any job quota card to inspect:
   - Monthly Net Salary (in EUR and NGN)
   - Accommodation terms (Free company housing included)
   - Processing duration (typically 60 to 90 business days)
4. Click **"Apply for Permit Quota"** or **"Check Eligibility"** to submit your CV and qualifications for initial employer review.

## 6. CLIENT PORTAL & APPLICATION TRACKING

Clients who have submitted an application or booked an airline itinerary can track status in real-time:

2. Enter your **Application Reference ID** or **6-Character Flight PNR**.

## 7. CURRENCY CONVERTER USAGE

Located in the top utility bar of every page is the **Currency Toggle**:
